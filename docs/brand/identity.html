Identity study · Candle Rush · Robinhood Chain

Five marks, one candle.

Candle Rush already owns a shape nobody else in the arcade does: a body with a wick through it. Every concept below is built from that one primitive and nothing else — no rockets, no coins, no upward squiggle. Five directions, three colourways each, all of them legible at sixteen pixels.

The primitive

One shape does all the work

01
Corner radius is 28% of the body width. Held across every mark, so a candle from one concept can stand next to a candle from another without looking borrowed.
02
The wick is always 15% of the body, at 50% opacity. It reads as a wick rather than a pole, and it is the first thing to disappear cleanly when the mark gets small.
03
Green is the candle you are on. Gold is the one that has not closed.The same rule the HUD already follows, so the logo and the game are speaking one language.
04
Never two candles in one lockup. If the icon carries a candle, the wordmark is plain. If the wordmark carries one — concept 02 — there is no icon.
01

The Ladder

Recommended

Three candles stepping up, and the last wick climbing out of the frame.

The game stopped being ninety seconds and became a ladder of levels, each one faster than the last. This is the only mark here that says that.

Read it left to right and it is progress: a candle you have left behind, the one you are on, the one that has not closed yet. The top wick breaks the bounding box on purpose — the run is not finished.

Where it goes

02

Wordspace Wick

Wordmark

The space between the two words is a candle. No icon, no lockup, no gap to fill.

CANDLE and RUSH are set at the display weight the game already uses, and the word space between them is replaced by a single green candle at cap height.

It is the cheapest thing a brand can own: the name and the mark are the same object, so every time somebody types the name they are half way to drawing it.

Doji Seal

Token

One doji in a ring: open equals close, long against short, the tape undecided.

A doji is the candle that has not made up its mind — the exact moment the player is in when they choose Continue or Back.

Circular, so it survives every avatar crop, every chain explorer, every wallet row. If Candle Rush ever mints anything, it already has a face.

The Hop

Arcade

The arc between two candle tops — the verb, not the setting.

Every other mark here draws the chart. This one draws the thing the player actually does: leave one candle, land on the next.

The dotted trail is the game's own jump arc, and the block at the apex is the trader mid-flight. It is the friendliest of the five, and the least financial.

Usage

Four rules worth holding

    Do

  • ✓Give the mark clear space equal to the width of one candle body on every side.
  • ✓Swap to the small cut at 32px. It is a different file for a reason.
  • ✓Put the one-colour version on photography, merch and anything printed in a single pass.
  • ✓Let the night ground be the default. The product is played after dark.

    Do not

  • ✕Re-colour the candles. Green up and red down is a convention older than this game.
  • ✕Add a stroke, a bevel, or a drop shadow to make it "pop" on a busy background. Change the background.
  • ✕Set the wordmark in anything but Bricolage 800 — or better, use the outlined file.
  • ✕Stretch a lockup to fill a box. Scale it, then leave the space empty.
