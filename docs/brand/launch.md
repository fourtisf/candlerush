## Do not say

The fastest ways to lose the room, in rough order of how often they happen.

- **Any price target, floor, or "we're early".** It converts the audience you want into the
  audience you don't, and it never comes back.
- **"Play to earn."** The in-game balance is points. It is not the token, and saying
  otherwise is the one claim that turns a bad week into a legal one. If the two are ever
  wired together, say so on the day it ships and not before.
- **"Audited", "KYC'd", "partnered with"** unless each is a document you can link.
- **Fake screenshots** — holder counts, volume, a leaderboard with names that are not real.
  Every one of these is checkable, and someone will check.
- **Engagement farming.** Reply-for-reply, follow-for-follow, giveaway ladders. The account
  is for a game that works; farmed followers will not play it and will not hold it.
- **Arguing with critics.** Answer once, factually, with something they can verify. Then
  stop. The reply is not for the person you are replying to — it is for the fifty people
  reading it.
